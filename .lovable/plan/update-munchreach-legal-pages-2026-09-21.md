# Update MunchReach legal pages

## Scope
Update only the nine existing legal pages and preserve their current URLs, effective-date structure, design, navigation, and footer links:

- Privacy Policy
- Sending Policy
- Terms of Service
- Refund Policy
- Acceptable Use
- Cookie Policy
- Data Processing Agreement
- Remove My Data
- Compliance

## Content changes
- Reframe MunchReach consistently as an email outreach and campaign-management platform built around Email Accounts, Connected Mailboxes, Campaigns, Sequences, Leads and Contacts, Recipients, Email Messages, replies, Analytics, and the Unified Inbox.
- Expand the Privacy Policy and DPA definitions to accurately cover customer-provided lead/contact records, campaign and message content, mailbox connection data, conversations, sending activity, analytics, billing, support, and integrations while preserving privacy rights, retention, security, data-sharing, subprocessor, transfer, and breach language.
- Refocus the Sending Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, Terms, and Compliance page on lawful sending, authorization to use connected mailboxes, sender identity, suppression, unsubscribes, bounce and complaint handling, sending limits, abuse prevention, and customer responsibility.
- Keep the Refund and Cookie policies focused on their existing purposes, removing only irrelevant legacy sending-infrastructure wording.
- Expand Remove My Data explanations to cover account, lead/contact, campaign, conversation, connected-mailbox, and activity records, including legally required retention and suppression records.

## Domain and DNS treatment
- Remove domain/DNS language where it is legacy product framing or presented as the primary customer workflow.
- Preserve narrow, accurate references where technically or legally relevant: recipient-address validation, lookalike-domain abuse, mailbox authentication records such as SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and unavoidable third-party email-service constraints.
- Do not expose internal email-delivery provider names or describe internal routing.
