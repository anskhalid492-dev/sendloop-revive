# Rebuild the existing About page around the founder story

## Outcome
Rebuild `/about` in place as a personal, first-person founder narrative. Keep the existing MunchReach header, footer, route, global typography, branding, and design system; create no second page and change no other page.

## Page structure
1. Compact editorial opening with “THE STORY BEHIND MUNCHREACH,” the supplied headline, supporting line, and a visual 2020 story marker.
2. “Dropped Out. Failed. Kept Building.” as an honest founder introduction that does not equate dropping out with success.
3. A 2020-to-MunchReach story spine covering the client acquisition agency, failed businesses, lessons, continued hands-on outbound work, and the transition from agency work to software.
4. “6+ Years of Learning Outbound the Hard Way” explained through the real moving parts of outbound rather than a feature list.
5. A dominant “30M+ Cold Emails Sent” chapter framed as accumulated experience, not social proof or a performance claim.
6. A visual fragmented-workflow chapter showing the disconnected tools that motivated one connected system.
7. “Why I Built MunchReach” using only existing product capabilities: leads, campaigns, sequences, AI personalization, follow-ups, sending accounts, Unified Inbox, CRM, analytics, and automation.
8. “Built From Scratch” and “Nobody Sees the Part That Happens After the Launch,” grounded in the real work of building and improving software.
9. Two prominent founder quotes using the supplied wording.
10. Five founder principles, followed by “This Is Just the Beginning.”
11. “Thanks for Being Here.” with “Try MunchReach” and “Explore the Platform” actions.

## Visual direction
- Follow the selected founder editorial narrative: left-aligned chapters, large type, purposeful asymmetry, generous whitespace, restrained blue/violet brand treatments, and a clear story spine.
- Use existing MunchReach interface screenshots as truthful visual evidence; do not create or imply a founder portrait.
- Keep cards limited to product fragments, principles, and other genuinely framed content.
- Add subtle reveal and timeline motion using the existing animation pattern, with reduced-motion support.
- Make the complete narrative comfortable on mobile and desktop, with stable image and type proportions.

## Content and safety guardrails
- Use only the founder facts and numbers supplied in the request.
- Do not include the old invented agency size, customer count, delivered-email count, inbox-placement rate, testimonial, team claims, awards, revenue, funding, investors, clients, or unsupported outcomes.
- Keep the voice first-person, conversational, grounded, and free of corporate or motivational-guru language.
- Update only the About page metadata to accurately describe the rebuilt founder story.

## Verification
- Check the rebuilt page at desktop and mobile widths for hierarchy, wrapping, overlap, image rendering, motion behavior, header/footer continuity, and CTA destinations.
- Confirm the page route and canonical remain `/about`, the metadata is complete, no unsupported claims remain, and the existing site navigation still works.
- Run focused static checks through the project harness and a browser pass before reporting completion.
